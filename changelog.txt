Version: 0.1.4
Date: 2026-11-11
  Features:
    - Feed paths view: the paths from a transformer's sources, coloured by line loss.
    - Big transmission pole: a big electric pole with no supply area.
    - Total upstream loss: everything lost between the generators and a transformer (lines, inefficiency, core loss).
    - Hover information, set per transformer: "Loss upstream" (default) and "Loss downstream".
  Changes:
    - Transformer panel redesigned: Upstream and Downstream with the same layout, power first, tooltips with the live figures.
    - Per-player setting to show all panel power figures in one unit.
    - The panel opens with whether nearest-first matching and machine weighting are on, and how many transformers or generators feed the transformer.
    - "Show" on a worst route circles its source and sink in red for 10 s.
    - Grid diagram diamonds are coloured by the total upstream loss.
    - Power figures rounded to three significant digits.
    - "Line loss" consumers show the big pole icon in the electric network statistics.
    - Per-player setting to show input - output next to total loss, for checking.
  Bugfixes:
    - Generator-fed transformers showed no upstream line loss after a feeding transformer was removed.
    - Stale nearest-first results could still be used for the upstream figures.

Version: 0.1.3
Date: 2026-11-10
  Features:
    - Cumulative line loss in the transformer panel, under the last segment: the line losses of every segment between the generators and that transformer, compounded, 1 - (1 - L1) x (1 - L2) x ...; transformer inefficiency and core loss are not counted. Every line's tooltip now defines the value.
    - Hovering a transformer shows Drawing and Line loss (of the last segment), coloured with the same thresholds as the grid diagram's diamonds.
  Changes:
    - Machine load is spread only over the poles that can power machines: huge poles and fuses have no supply area, so they carry power without diluting the average.
    - The heatmap and "power lost per pole" views are merged into one "line-loss map" shortcut: dot colour = line loss of the power reaching the pole, dot size = power lost delivering its machine load. A key bound to the heatmap keeps working; one bound to "power lost per pole" has to be bound again.
    - Removed the legends: the legend window of the map views, and the legend in the grid power panel.
    - Transformer panel: sections renamed Upstream and Downstream, "Transformer inefficiency" shortened to "Inefficiency", output shown without the after-line-loss figure (already in the downstream line); upstream and downstream used throughout the panel tooltips, settings descriptions and documentation.
    - The transformer panel no longer shows distances; the worst-routes list keeps them, with a tooltip explaining that they are resistance-weighted (tiles of big-pole wire).
    - Power Overload's high- and low-priority transformers are hidden (recipes removed and locked); ones already built keep working. Their priority does not fit how this mod splits a network's supply between transformers.
  Optimizations:
    - Wire searches read each pole's name once and look up its resistance, type and supply area per prototype, instead of asking the game several times per pole.
    - The text under a hovered transformer is recomputed when the selection changes and twice a second, not every tick.
    - Less work per tick while building: networks waiting for a new search are matched against stored network ids.
  Bugfixes:
    - A transformer feeding routes of very different lengths was charged too little: it averaged the routes' losses before converting them to power lost. It is now charged exactly the sum of each route's loss, P x L / (1 - L), so the grid diagram's routes add up to the burn. The downstream line loss shown is that total as a share of the output.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-09
  Features:
    - First release, split out of a Power Overload fork as an add-on mod.
    - Distance-based line losses between transformers, weighted by pole type (resistance from each pole's power rating).
    - Line loss capped at 95% per route; a source pays the flow-weighted average of its routes.
    - Transformers fed directly by generators pay for the wires to them, weighted by each generator's rated output, drawn by a "Line loss" consumer on the generator network. Several transformers drawing from the same generators are paired with them by the same balancing as "feed nearest consumers first".
    - Losses are drawn by hidden "Line loss" (downstream network) and "Transf. ineff." (upstream network) consumers, visible in the electric network statistics.
    - Own transformer efficiency setting (default 99%); Power Overload's efficiency should be set to 100%.
    - Opt-in "weight machines by their live load": poles weighted by the machines in their supply area and what each machine type draws right now.
    - Transformer core loss (default 0.1% of its rating, while switched on). A transformer is rated by the weaker of its two sides: the highest-rated pole wired to each side, and the smaller of the two.
    - Hovering a transformer shows the power it draws and the line loss of the power arriving at it.
    - Panel next to an opened transformer: upstream and downstream line loss, transformer inefficiency, power drawn and output.
    - "Feed nearest consumers first" sharing (on by default); machines are split by pole ownership (each pole charged to its electrically nearest transformer), so feeding a network from several sides cuts the loss of its machines between transformers on the same network.
    - "Line resistance" row in power pole tooltips.
    - Grid diagram on the map (shortcut), with an energy-balance panel and the routes losing the most power (button opens the map there).
    - "Power lost per pole" map view (shortcut): dots sized and coloured by the watts lost delivering each pole's machine load.
    - Pole heatmap on the map (shortcut): each pole coloured by the line loss of the power reaching it.
    - Distance searches re-run only when a network changes (pole built or removed, networks merged or split, transformer added or removed), plus a slow background pass.
